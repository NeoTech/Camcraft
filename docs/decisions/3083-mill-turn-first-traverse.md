## #3083 - On a mill-turn, the mill kinds answer to the first-traverse laws of a mill

- **What was decided** (taken unattended; the maintainer can change it):
  1. **The same laws, from the post's real order.** A mill-turn posts a milling tool through the mill post: `posting::default_post_for` gives `MillTurn` the built-in Fanuc mill post, whose first move is `G00 Z` to the clearance (`traverseZ`), then `G00 X Y` at that height (`xyFirst`, `assets/posts/fanuc.cps`). That is the order that the first-traverse laws read. So `first_traverse::mill_order(kind, lathe_tool, params)` is true on a mill and on a mill-turn for a milling tool, and the three calls in `generate.rs` (`turned_axis` and the two `judge` calls) read it in place of `is_mill`. The planar, 3-D, hole (axial and cross) and multi-axis kinds on a mill-turn get the upright law, the 3+2 table law (#2987, #3081) and the tip law (#2965), with no change to the laws.
  2. **The premise of the maintainer (decisions #2965, #2987, #3081).** The unknown start is at the top of the Z travel (the tool-change height). **NOT covered:** a program that starts with the tool low near the work, the chuck or the table, for example after a manual jog. The operator must send the machine to its tool-change height before the program starts.
  3. **The steel of the mill-turn.** The laws read the steel that the setup holds: each tagged workholding model (a chuck, a tailstock or a steady rest model, split turning and fixed by `fixture::workholding_for`) and the box from the stated X/Y/Z travel. The machine file states no chuck, tailstock or turret body (`millturn.mch` has none), so an untagged chuck is not judged - as an untagged vise on a mill is not. The turret holds the tools; the tool's own stack is not steel to its first traverse.
  4. **A rotary kind on a mill-turn stays out (follow-up #3096, P1).** There a rotary kind (`Mode::Rotary`) turns the bar on the spindle C under a radial live tool. Its first rapid comes from the turret index position, not from an upright tool over an unknown X/Y. With a vertical C, the swept law of #2965 reads the start at its height along the spindle against the bar's top, so a radial contour that cuts under the bar top is refused (for example the C-axis tests, whose clearance Z 25 stands under a 34 mm bar stock). The right law is the live-tool reading of #3077 (`verify::live_first_traverse`); #3096 extends it to the rotary kinds after #3077 merges. Before #3083 no law judged these kinds either, so nothing that was refused now posts.
  5. **The struck-link planner stays mill-only** (`generate.rs`, `fixture_route::plan_links`). On a mill-turn the post gate judges each link against the steel (`posting::fixture_problems` -> `fixture_struck::op_hits`, no kind gate) and refuses a struck one, so no link posts unjudged. The planner would route the link over the steel, which changes the mill-turn's posted program: an enhancement, not this P1 fix.
  6. **The mesh steel stays mill-only** (`generate.rs`, `mesh_steel`). The mesh only acquits a box hit (`verify::mesh_refined`), so the box verdict that a mill-turn keeps is the stricter one. No hit can pass.
  7. **Mill-turn caches read Stale once.** The post gate trusts the warnings that a cache carries. `current_stamp` writes the word `first-traverse-mill-turn-3083` for each operation that `first_traverse::newly_judged_on` names (a judged mill kind on a mill-turn setup). No mill's stamp moves, and the global salt stays. No shipped example or catalog project mounts a mill-turn (scout read of `examples/` and `docs/tester-kits/`), so none reads Stale.
  8. **The overlap with #3077.** #3077 routes the mill-turn's cross drill into the upright `judge` with its own flag (`mill_post_cross`). `mill_order` already includes that drill, so the merged flag is `mill_order || mill_post_cross`, and its live-tool law stays beside it.
- **Why.** Prime Directive: a first rapid through the stock or the steel must be refused by name. A mill-turn's mill kinds post the same first move as a mill's, so they must answer to the same laws.
- **Considered and dropped.**
  - Change `is_mill` in `job.rs` to include the mill-turn: it also gates the mesh steel, the planner and the cross-drill refusal ("a mill with no turret"), and each of those must stay as it is.
  - Judge the rotary kinds with the mill premise: it refuses the legal C-axis live-tool work of each shipped test, a false refusal for each program.
  - Refuse every mill kind on a mill-turn until a mill-turn law exists: a refusal of legal work, and the mill laws apply as they are.
  - A lathe post on a mill-turn mill op: `default_post_for` gives the mill post. A lathe post can send X first; that order is not judged here (as #3077 states).
- **Cost to change later**: low. `mill_order` and `newly_judged_on` in `operation/first_traverse.rs`, one stamp word.
