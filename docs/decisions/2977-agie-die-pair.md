## #2977 - Two through-openings in one native Agie die job

**Decided.** Match each selected rim to an opening in the same modeled plate. Require exactly two openings and two disjoint compensated cutting and threading areas. Keep each work's threading point, inward cut side, capture and separation.

**Why.** The two operations protect the same retained material. Matching each selected boundary against the complete vertical prism checks that the cut goes through the plate. Disjoint envelopes require the operations to use different openings and leave a clear band between their cuts.

**Alternatives and cost.** Mixed punch and die work would need interference checks against the entire retained die plate. Additional openings would need complete one-to-one rim matching, clearance checks between every pair and verification of the controller's release sequence. Both extensions can use the same resource map after those checks exist.
