## #2980 - Independent punch groups on one Agie plate

**Decision.** Accept up to nine separately captured modeled punches on one 20 mm setup with the qualified QN2 process. Bind one native work and one ISO resource to each complete source model. Compare each work's full rough-cut, threading and lead envelope with every other work before publishing the bundle.

**Reason.** The control schedules the profile passes for each punch, then its separation passes. The released punches must remain independently supported. A clearance test limited to consecutive works can miss a collision between the first and last punches.

**Alternatives and cost.** Nine works fit the single-digit ISO suffix and resolved nine distinct resources in the controller. Its 72 cut blocks kept all nine profile and separation paths apart. A tenth work needs a new filename budget and qualification of its resource and quality bindings. Mixed die and punch groups need additional checks against the retained die plate. The bounded pairwise comparisons cost at most 36 checks in the supported range.
