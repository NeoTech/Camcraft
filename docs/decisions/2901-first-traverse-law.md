## #2901 - The opening rapid starts over its entry; the first traverse needs a clearance plane over the stock and every fixture

- **What was decided** (the coordinator's decision on the issue, built as written; the maintainer can change it):
  1. **Seat.** The opening rapid of a clearance-seeded path starts over its own entry X/Y on the clearance plane (`cl::Toolpath::seat_approach(seed)`). It no longer starts over the WCS origin (#2382's seat, reversed) or over the frame zero. The toolpath, the CL `START` line, the simulation, the viewport lines, the fixture checks and the link router see no travel from a point that the machine never visits. The end of the rapid does not change, so no posted block changes.
  2. **First-traverse law.** After a tool change the control does not know its X/Y. The post sends Z to the plane that the path starts on, then X/Y at that plane. That one traverse can cross any point of the setup. The law (`sim::verify::first_traverse_low`) requires the start height of the path to be at or above the top of the start stock and the top of each fixture, in the frame of the operation. A 3+2 plane reads the eight corners of each box along its tool axis. If the start is lower, the result is the blocking verdict `Warning::FirstTraverseLow { at: 0, height_mm, top_mm, fixture }`. Its text gives the clearance height and the height to clear, for example: "the first rapid (move 0) comes from an unknown X/Y at the clearance height Z8.000, under the stock top at Z10.000 - raise the clearance height above Z10.000". The post gate puts the operation name first and writes no file.
     - The stock top is the full start stock (the stock box, and the top of the operation's material), never the in-process stock. The start X/Y is not known, so no line can be read again against what an earlier operation cut.
     - The steel is the setup's fixture boxes (`fixture::named_boxes_for`: the tagged fixtures, grown by their chord error, then the machine table). A 3+2 plane does not read the machine table: its box is a slab under the whole travel (`MAX_FIXTURE_DIM` wide), so a tilted axis reads a far edge of that slab (about 1e6 mm) and not a top that the plane can clear: with the table read, every indexed and 3+2 operation was refused (`fourth_axis_pose`, `index_swing`, `first_4axis_part`). The stock and the tagged fixtures are read in every frame. Upright, the table top is under the stock and is never the higher top. The coordinator accepted this narrowing. What it leaves unjudged: the first traverse of a 3+2 operation against the machine table (a tilted plane drops toward the table on its low side, for example about 0.36 mm for each mm of travel at B 20 deg). #2987 (P1) owns that gap (one design: clip the table box to the machine travel before the corner reading). The rotary, multi-axis and cross-drill kinds are #2965.
     - A start at the top passes (within `Z_TOL`), as the rapid law passes a traverse at the stock top.
     - `app` passes the extents and selects the kinds (`operation/first_traverse.rs`). The verdict is a `Warning` that blocks the post like the other verdicts. MCP names it `first_traverse_low`.
  3. **Scope.** Milling paths on a mill without a rotary axis: the 2D and 3D milling kinds and the vertical hole kinds, upright or 3+2. Not in this law: the multi-axis kinds, the setups with a rotary axis, the cross drill, turning, plasma, wire EDM and sinker EDM. Turning, plasma and EDM read their clearance on other axes or surfaces (a lathe station, a torch height, a wire). The rapid laws and the simulation judge the moves of their paths. The rotary, multi-axis and cross-drill kinds have no first-traverse law yet: #2965 (P1) owns it.
  4. **Cache.** The op stamp salt changes from `camcraft-op-stamp-v3` to `v4`. A saved cache holds the rapid from the origin and was judged without the new law. It reads Stale once, and the post refuses it until a regenerate.
  5. **Words.** The door word at creation (`clearance_stock::creation_advice`) and the "Clearance in stock:" note no longer offer "face the stock first" as a fix. A face ahead does not lift the first traverse over the start stock, so that fix is now false. Both say: raise Clearance above the stock top. The guide's troubleshooting page (`site/content/guide/reference/troubleshooting.md`) drops the same advice and gets an entry for the new message: what it means and how to fix it.
- **Reuse check.** The existing laws were read first. `RapidInStock` (rapid law (d)) judges a rapid along its known line. The first traverse has no known line, so the rapid law cannot judge it. The "Clearance in stock:" note names the cause of a convicted `RapidInStock` and never blocks. The door word is a word at creation, not a verdict. The `grblhal` and `tinyg` post scripts refuse a clearance under `StockZTop`, but only in those two posts and only for the stock. No existing law blocks this case on every path. Thus one new verdict is added, and the old laws stay.

- **Known cost (accepted).** An operation whose clearance plane is under the stock top or a fixture top on purpose is refused, for example a contour after a facing pass took the whole top down. The message says how to repair it: raise the clearance height.
- **What was considered and dropped**:
  - The post sends X/Y first at a safe height, then Z: each NC golden changes. A larger change for its own issue.
  - No check (the seat alone): the old seat convicted a Z 8 plane under a Z 10 stock only by accident, on a line that the machine does not travel. Without a law the program posts, and the first traverse can go through the stock or a clamp.
  - The in-process stock for the top: the start X/Y is not known, so an uncut corner anywhere can be on the line.
  - The machine table in a 3+2 frame: its slab box gives a "top" a million millimetres away along a tilted axis and refuses each indexed operation.
  - The rule in `camcraft-motion` or `camcraft-cl`: the verdict type (`Warning`) and the steel (`FixtureBox`) are in `camcraft-sim`, with the other rapid and fixture laws. The rule goes there, and no crate dependency is added.
- **Cost to change later**: low. One verdict variant and one law function. A post that writes X/Y first at a safe height can turn the law off for its machines with one condition in `operation::first_traverse::answers`.
