## #2880 - Turned hidden-rest parity on revolved meridian edges and open-recess bounds

**What was decided.** Fix both false-rest readings on a turned part with a neck and a modelled thread at their geometric causes in `camcraft-sim`:

1. In `crates/sim/src/solid_rest.rs`, record each boundary claim's orientation-free CCW XY angular sector (packed into `Claim.sector: [f32; 2]`, keeping `Claim` at 40 bytes) and settle multiple non-coplanar bare-boundary plane groups at one Z level by angular multiplicity: emit 1 crossing when any open angular sector around the column has odd group multiplicity (a single manifold surface crossed on a revolved meridian edge, cone apex or rim), and 0 when every sector has even multiplicity (a grazing V-root or overhang fold). Single coplanar groups stay 1, keeping the knife-edge `Open` verdict on flush stacked shells.
2. In `crates/sim/src/stock_sim/scan_turned.rs`, replace the `±reach`-inflated recess and band check in `TurnedBand::excuses` with strict overlap against the open recess `(p, roof)`. A turning pass whose segments never enter the open recess `(p, roof)` (stopping at the step face `Z20..Z60` or starting at the roof `Z63..Z78` of a `Z60..Z63` neck) does not own that recess's floor; any pass that enters or bridges the open recess still owns it and convicts.

**Why.** On a 64-segment revolved thread, grid columns on the 45-degree meridian (`x == y`) hit the shared edge of two non-coplanar tilted flank facets at the same Z. The old plane-group merge only merged coplanar boundary claims, so each flank emitted two crossings instead of one; across an even number of flanks that stayed `Paired` while inverting solid and air inside each V-groove and exposing the downward-facing upper flank as a hidden top (`0.7028 mm rest`). Separately, inflating a recess `[lo, hi]` by `±reach` (`±(r + floor)`) made a cone pass ending cleanly at `Z60` (and a thread pass starting at `Z63`) claim the `Z60..Z63` neck step face even though no non-gouging tangent ball centre can sit outside `(60, 63)` (`0.2435 mm rest`).

**What was considered and dropped.**
- Filtering zero-width gaps (`roof == hi`) in `scan_hidden.rs` alone: rejected because it leaves `solid_rest::column_crossings` inverted on every revolved meridian edge (affecting any closed cone or threaded bar, and hiding real crossings inside the inverted interval).
- Symbolic perturbation (Simulation of Simplicity) in `solid_rest`: rejected because it changes the documented contract on box boundary edges (`columns_on_edges_and_vertices_stay_even` and `flush_stacked_shells_are_reported_not_guessed`).
- Asymmetric recess inflation (strict on `lo`, `+reach` on `hi`): rejected because it would still block a Profile Finish window starting cleanly at the top of a neck (`Z63..Z78`) with false rest from the neck floor 3 mm below it.
- Adding a turning-specific bypass for modelled threads: rejected because the Minkowski offset (`profile_offset`) and `Access::reachable` already agree on every true upward-facing thread flank once `solid_rest` reports true parity.

**Cost to change later.** Both changes are self-contained inside `camcraft-sim` (`solid_rest.rs`, `scan_turned.rs`, `scan_hidden.rs`) with no public API or schema change; adjusting either law later touches only those files and their unit/integration pins.
