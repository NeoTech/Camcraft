# HEIDENHAIN TNC manuals: inventory

Collected 2026-11-26 from the HEIDENHAIN TNCguide portal (`https://content.heidenhain.de/doku/tnc_guide/html/en/index/1242135142456/1242135142456.html`, portal edition "TNCguide (10/2026)"). English editions only. Programming manuals only: Klartext / conversational, DIN/ISO, cycles (machining and measuring), setup, and the "Overview of New and Modified Software Functions" documents. Not downloaded on purpose: other languages, smarT.NC pilots, training courses, CHM help archives, TNC7 "complete edition" PDFs, TNCdiag, machine-parameter overviews, NC error message lists.

The manuals were downloaded once (about 3.5 GB) for text extraction with `pdftotext -layout`. They are not part of this repository. Use the source URL to get a manual.

**Terms:** the portal pages show only "© HEIDENHAIN 2026" and link "Conditions of Use" to `http://www.heidenhain.de/de_EN/company/terms-of-use/`. That page (and heidenhain.com) answered HTTP 403 to automated requests, so the terms were not read. The PDFs carry HEIDENHAIN copyright. Use them as behaviour facts only; quote short phrases with page numbers.

Column "date" is the most frequent `mm/yyyy` in the manual text (normally the edition date in the page footer). "pages" is the PDF page count. Page numbers elsewhere in these notes are PDF pages unless the text says "printed page".
