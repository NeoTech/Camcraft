A toolpath is only as good as the machining decisions behind it. These pages
explain the challenges every CAM job meets, in general machining terms, and
then say plainly what CamCraft does about each one: which setting carries it,
what the program gate checks, and what is left to you at the machine.

The principles are general machining knowledge: the standard formulas and
the habits a shop passes on. They are not feeds-and-speeds tables. CamCraft
does not know your machine, your material lot or your fixture, so where a
page says "check", the check is yours. Where CamCraft has no feature for
something, the page says so.
